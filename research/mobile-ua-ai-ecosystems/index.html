What's Inside

Mobile user acquisition is being rebuilt on a new foundation. Rising costs, privacy constraints, and AI ecosystem emergence are not cyclical shifts — they are structural ones. This paper breaks down exactly what's changed and what to do about it.

  • The economics of mobile UA in 2025–2026 — $78B in global spend, CPI benchmarks by platform and region
  • How Apple Intelligence and Google Gemini are changing app discovery from keyword matching to AI-native, intent-based search
  • Why generative AI apps became the fastest-growing acquisition category at $824M in spend
  • The privacy reset: SKAdNetwork, Privacy Sandbox, and why first-party data is your new competitive moat
  • The creative engine: AI-powered ad production at 40–100+ variants per month
  • Retention-first acquisition: why Google now ranks apps by retention, not install volume
  • A three-horizon strategic framework for founders and executives navigating the shift
